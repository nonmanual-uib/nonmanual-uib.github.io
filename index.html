Fundamentals of formal properties of nonmanuals: a quantitative approach

NONMANUAL is a research project funded by the European Research Council. The project is dedicated to the study of nonmanual markers in sign languages. We combine sign language linguistics, statistical methods and computational approaches to investigate how facial expressions, head movements, and body postures contribute to meaning.

Project aims

  • Create a quantitative formal typology of nonmanual markers across five diverse sign languages
  • Build computational tools for detecting and classifying nonmanual markers
  • Create open resources for the sign language research community
  • Foster collaboration through workshops and conferences
