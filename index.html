Fundamentals of formal properties of nonmanuals: a quantitative approach

NONMANUAL is a research project dedicated to the study of non-manual markers in sign languages. We combine sign-language linguistics, statistical methods, and computational approaches to investigate how facial expressions, head movements, and body postures contribute to meaning.

Project aims

  • Build computational tools for detecting and classifying non-manual markers
  • Create open resources for the sign-language research community
  • Foster international collaboration through workshops and conferences
